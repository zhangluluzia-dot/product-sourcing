# Marketplace and Supplier-Source Notes

Use these notes to interpret listings and supplier claims. They do not replace verification on the live page or in current documents.

## Taobao and Tmall

- Taobao provides broad selection and independent sellers; Tmall brand and official flagship stores generally provide clearer identity and after-sales terms.
- Search cards often show the cheapest variant, coupon price, or a range. Open the listing and select the exact SKU.
- Delivery estimates can depend on the signed-in shipping address. Record whether the estimate is destination-specific.
- Image search is useful for visual matching, but similarity does not prove dimensions, material, performance, or authenticity.

## JD.com

- Distinguish JD self-operated, official flagship, and marketplace sellers.
- Separate dispatch speed from installation, scheduled delivery, and large-item services.
- Verify the selected region, voltage, warranty coverage, and service availability for appliances, electronics, equipment, or oversized goods.

## 1688

- Treat 1688 as supplier and wholesale sourcing. Capture MOQ, sample price, quantity tiers, customization, tooling, production lead time, packing, invoice terms, and domestic freight.
- A displayed price may apply only to a quantity tier, sample, deposit, component, unfinished product, or excluded accessory.
- Supplier badges and transaction history are signals, not substitutes for samples, written specifications, inspection, or contract review.

## Pinduoduo and Xianyu

- Pinduoduo prices, subsidies, group-buy conditions, and availability can be account- or app-specific. Verify the exact variant, seller, and return terms.
- Use Xianyu only when the user accepts used, open-box, display, discontinued, or individually sold goods. Capture condition, defects, missing parts, authenticity evidence, freight, and return limitations.

## International retail and distribution

- Confirm destination currency, tax, oversize freight, marketplace seller, voltage, warranty region, return shipping, and estimated arrival.
- Prefer manufacturer-authorized distributors when traceability, warranty, calibration, firmware, certification, or genuine parts matter.
- Distributor stock labels may refer to regional warehouses or backorders. Verify ship-from location and lead time.

## Direct manufacturers and B2B directories

- Distinguish manufacturer, trading company, distributor, and sourcing agent. Treat self-described status as a claim until supported.
- Capture business identity, location, years active, production scope, capacity, export markets, quality systems, audit or test evidence, references, MOQ, sample policy, tooling, customization, packaging, and payment terms.
- Alibaba, Made-in-China, Global Sources, and similar badges or verification programs are useful signals but do not eliminate sample, inspection, contract, payment, or logistics risk.
- For cross-border sourcing, separate origin delivery, consolidation, export documents, freight, duty, brokerage, and final-mile costs.
