# Industry Profiles

Use only the profile relevant to the current request. These checks supplement the shared sourcing workflow.

## FF&E, interiors, and construction materials

- Verify dimensions, tolerances, finish, color, material, construction, mounting, fire or slip ratings, maintenance, installation, spare parts, and room or project suitability.
- For reference-image matching, separate visual similarity from structural, dimensional, and material compliance.

## Electronics, appliances, and electrical equipment

- Verify model and region, voltage, frequency, plug, power, capacity, interfaces, firmware or software compatibility, certifications, warranty region, service network, and included accessories.
- Flag gray-market, counterfeit, discontinued, unsupported, or region-locked risks.

## Industrial components, tools, and equipment

- Verify manufacturer part number, drawing revision, dimensions and tolerances, material grade, load or performance ratings, interfaces, operating environment, calibration, consumables, spare parts, and technical documentation.
- Distinguish genuine, compatible, refurbished, and custom-made parts. Recommend a sample, first-article inspection, or qualified technical review when failure risk is material.

## Packaging, print, private label, OEM, and ODM

- Verify substrate, thickness or weight, dimensions, tolerances, print method, color standard, finish, artwork requirements, tooling, sample stages, MOQ, quantity tiers, defect allowance, packing, labeling, and intellectual-property responsibilities.
- Separate prototype, pre-production sample, and mass-production lead times.

## Hospitality, foodservice, and commercial equipment

- Verify capacity, throughput, power or gas requirements, drainage and ventilation, food-contact documentation, cleanability, installation, local service, consumables, spare parts, and warranty.
- Do not certify health, electrical, gas, or food-contact compliance; report documentation and gaps for qualified review.

## Consumer, office, and general business goods

- Verify exact variant, durability, compatibility, warranty, returns, recurring consumables, pack count, seller identity, delivery, and total cost at the requested quantity.
- Prefer straightforward retail sourcing when customization or supplier qualification is unnecessary.
